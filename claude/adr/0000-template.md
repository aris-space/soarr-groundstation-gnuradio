# NNNN — <short decision title>

> *One-sentence summary of the decision.*

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [ADR-XXXX](xxxx-....md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** <who>

## Context

What forces are at play? What problem or requirement triggered this decision?
Keep it factual — the situation, the constraints (mission, hardware, schedule),
and why a decision is needed now.

## Decision

What we decided to do, stated plainly.

## Alternatives considered

The options that were genuinely on the table and why each was rejected. An ADR
without real alternatives is usually not worth writing.

- **Option A** — … Rejected because …
- **Option B** — … Rejected because …

## Consequences

What becomes easier, harder, or riskier as a result. Include known warts and
follow-ups, not just the upside.

---

_Write an ADR only when the decision is (1) hard to reverse, (2) surprising
without context, and (3) the result of a real trade-off. If any of the three is
missing, it probably belongs in a doc or in CONTEXT.md, not here._
